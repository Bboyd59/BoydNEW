# Boyd Intelligence — design brief

## Design read
Prospective clients of a one-person California design + AI studio; the register is warm, confident, personal craft with serious technical capability. Sexy, luminous, never cold.

## Concept spine
Artifact/collectible: "the cast". Everything the studio makes is cast in warm glass. The whole site is a light study of ONE molten honey-glass artifact, the B, seen ever more closely as it forms. Sections present work, studio, and process as facets of the same cast object.

## Delivery tier
cinema (Lenis + GSAP, scroll chapters; the scroll-scrub film is the Tier-1 hero mechanic).

## Locked palette
- Base: sunlit warm white `#FFF9F0` (high-key, luminous; NOT beige/cream gray)
- Ink: deep umber `#2A1B10`
- Muted ink: `#8A6F58`
- Accent: honey amber `#C97F2E` (single accent, page-wide)
Defense: the user explicitly chose a honey-amber warm-glass identity ("warm and unique versus cold", picked "warm glass / honey-amber B"). This is molten glass and light, high-key and luminous, not the muted beige+brass craft family; user brand choice overrides the default ban.

## Locked type
Cabinet Grotesk (display, expressive grotesk) + Inter Tight (body). No serif, no mono.

## Animation mode: animated-website
- Journey shape: single-shot
- REVISION (luxury pass, user-directed): the film now opens in near-black espresso darkness with molten gold forging the B (chapters 1-3, champagne text on dark), sunlight blooms in during chapter 4, and the final chapter plus all sections are the bright sunlit world (umber ink). Palette gains deep espresso #1A1109 and champagne #F6E7CE for the dark act; gold #D9A441 as the film accent. Nav becomes a frosted deep-umber bar (works over both acts). Type scale raised one step site-wide.
- Journey (5 chapters over one ~15s film):
  1. Studio — "Intelligence, warmly made." / one-person studio in Upland, CA; brands, websites, custom AI by the same hands. (film: molten honey pool swirling)
  2. Craft — "Three ways in." / brand kit, website, or custom AI tool; clear scope, clear price, no retainers. tags: Brand from $999, Websites from $2,999, AI from $4,999 (film: liquid rising, stretching)
  3. Studio behind the name — "One set of hands." / designed, built, delivered by the same person; tested on the bleeding edge first. (film: the glass B forming)
  4. Process — "Scoped in fifteen minutes." / one short call sets scope and price; short rounds you can see and steer. (film: orbit, light blooming through the B)
  5. Begin — "It starts with a call." / finished files, a live site, or a working tool; yours outright. + Book-a-call action (film: resolved beauty frame, warm flare)
- World grammar: ONE continuous take, seamless sunlit warm-white studio void, molten honey-amber glass hero centered, apricot-gold light from above, soft caustics on the floor, locked exposure, slow constant motion, no cuts, no on-screen text. Copy sits in deep umber ink over the bright low-detail field.
- Mobile framing: B and molten action held center-safe; lighter mobile encode (<=720p).
- Delivery budget: <=32 MiB desktop clips, <=16 MiB mobile.
- How the journey enacts the spine: the scroll literally performs the casting, raw material to finished artifact, mirroring an engagement from inquiry to handover.

## Combinatorial pick (boards)
- Theme paradigm: Pristine Light (sunlit warm white, umber ink)
- Background character: solid with soft ambient warm-light depth
- Typography character: expressive display grotesk (Cabinet-like)
